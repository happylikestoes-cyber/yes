ABOUT REDWOOD

We're a game studio that believes play should be bold, loud, and unapologetically fun. We build experiences that millions come back to — every single day.

Our Story

From a spark to billions of plays.

Redwood Studios was founded with one mission: build games people can't stop talking about. What started as a small team with big ideas has grown into a studio with 15 live games and over 5.7 billion lifetime visits.

We obsess over the details — the feel of a button press, the dopamine hit of a reward, the moment a player laughs out loud. Every pixel, every sound, every mechanic is crafted to create joy.

Our Approach

Data-driven. Player-obsessed.

We combine creative instinct with rigorous analytics. Every update is informed by real player behaviour, and every new game is built on lessons learned from millions of sessions.

Our community isn't an afterthought — it's our engine. Player feedback shapes our roadmaps, fuels our ideas, and keeps us honest. We build for them, with them.

Our Values

WHAT WE STAND FOR

The principles that drive every decision we make.

01

Ship Fast, Learn Faster

We move at speed. Launch early, gather feedback, iterate relentlessly. Perfect is the enemy of live.

02

Players Over Metrics

Numbers matter, but people matter more. We optimise for smiles, not just session length.

03

Bold Bets Win

Safe is boring. We take swings on wild ideas because the best games come from the most unexpected places.

04

Transparency Always

We share openly — with our team, our community, and our partners. No hidden agendas, just honest building.

05

Craft in Every Pixel

Good enough isn't good enough. We sweat the details because players can feel the difference.

06

Grow Together

Everyone on the team has a voice. We invest in each other's growth because a stronger team makes stronger games.
